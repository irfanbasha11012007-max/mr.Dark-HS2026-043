## INSTITUTION PROFILE (Verified Facts)

- **Full Name:** Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology (MCET)
- **Type:** Autonomous, self-financing institution
- **Established:** 1998, to commemorate the 75th birthday of Arutchelvar Dr. N. Mahalingam
- **Location:** Pollachi, Coimbatore District, Tamil Nadu, India
- **Affiliation:** Anna University, Chennai
- **Approvals:** All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi
- **Recognition:** University Grants Commission (UGC)
- **Accreditation:** NAAC (A++ grade) and NBA (National Board of Accreditation)
- **Group Background:** Part of the educational sector of the ABT & Sakthi Group of Companies
- **Website:** mcet.in

### Departments Offered
- Automobile Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Computer Science and Engineering
- Electrical and Electronics Engineering
- Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering
- Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering
- Information Technology

### Academic Programmes
- **UG:** B.E. / B.Tech (4 years) — admission via TNEA
- **PG:** M.E. / M.Tech, MCA (2 years) — admission via TANCET (ME) / TANCA (MCA)
- **Doctoral:** Ph.D (research-based, typically up to 6 years)

### International Collaborations
MCET has MoUs with international universities including Edith Cowan University (Australia), University of Cape Breton (Canada), University of Manitoba (Canada), Cambrian College of Applied Arts and Technology (Canada), and Northumbria University (UK).

### Campus Facilities
- Central Library
- Separate hostels for boys and girls
- Medical facility
- Transport facility
- Gymnasium
- Training & Placement Cell

## PART 1: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### General / Institution

**Q1. What is Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology?**
A. MCET is an autonomous, self-financing engineering institution located in Pollachi, Coimbatore District, Tamil Nadu, established in 1998 and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

**Q2. Is MCET accredited?**
A. Yes, MCET is accredited by NAAC (A++ grade) and NBA, and is approved by AICTE and recognized by UGC.

**Q3. What departments/branches does MCET offer?**
A. Automobile Engineering, Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering, and Information Technology.

**Q4. What programmes does MCET offer?**
A. Undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech), Postgraduate (M.E./M.Tech, MCA), and Doctoral (Ph.D) programmes.

**Q5. Does MCET have international collaborations?**
A. Yes, MCET has MoUs with universities including Edith Cowan University (Australia), University of Cape Breton (Canada), University of Manitoba (Canada), Cambrian College (Canada), and Northumbria University (UK).

### Attendance & Academics

**Q6. What is the minimum attendance requirement?**
A. Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for semester examinations.

**Q7. What happens if a student falls below the minimum attendance?**
A. Students below the required attendance threshold may not be permitted to sit for semester examinations and may need to seek condonation as per institutional policy.

### College Timings

**Q8. What are the college working hours?**
A. MCET operates Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

**Q9. Is the college open on Saturdays?**
A. Not covered in this knowledge base — please check with the administration directly.

### Library

**Q10. How many books can a student borrow from the library?**
A. Students can borrow up to 3 books at a time.

**Q11. How long can library books be borrowed?**
A. Books can be borrowed for a period of 14 days.

**Q12. Can reference books be taken home?**
A. No. Reference books are for in-library use only.

**Q13. What is the library opening time?**
A. The library opens at 8:00 AM.

**Q14. What happens if a book is returned late?**
A. Not covered in this knowledge base.

### Examinations

**Q15. How early should students report to the examination hall?**
A. At least 15 minutes before the scheduled examination time.

**Q16. Are mobile phones allowed in the examination hall?**
A. No, mobile phones are strictly prohibited in the examination hall.

**Q17. What documents should a student carry to the exam hall?**
A. Hall ticket and college ID card.

### Leave Policy

**Q18. How should planned leave be submitted?**
A. Planned leave must be submitted through the student portal in advance.

**Q19. How many working days does a student have to report emergency leave?**
A. Emergency leave must be reported and regularized within two working days.

### Student Clubs

**Q20. What types of student clubs are available?**
A. Technical, cultural, sports, and social service clubs.

**Q21. Is club membership compulsory?**
A. No, club membership is voluntary.

### Hostel

**Q22. What should students carry while entering the hostel?**
A. Their hostel identity card.

**Q23. Does MCET provide separate hostels for boys and girls?**
A. Yes, MCET provides separate hostel facilities for boys and girls.

### Student Portal & IT Support

**Q24. Who handles technical issues related to the student portal?**
A. The IT support team.

**Q25. Are students required to keep their student portal passwords confidential?**
A. Yes, students must keep their portal passwords confidential and not share them.

### Admissions (General)

**Q26. How are B.Tech admissions conducted at MCET?**
A. B.Tech/BE admissions are based on TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions).

**Q27. How are MCA admissions conducted?**
A. MCA admissions are based on TANCA.

**Q28. How are M.E./M.Tech admissions conducted?**
A. M.E./M.Tech admissions are based on TANCET, followed by merit/counselling.

### Principal & Placements

**Q29. Who is the current principal of MCET?**
A. Dr. A. Rathinavelu is the current principal of MCET.

**Q30. What companies visit MCET for placements?**
A. Top recruiters include Microsoft, Zoho, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, and NTT Data.

**Q31. What was the highest salary package in recent placements?**
A. The highest salary package reached Rs 51 LPA.

**Q32. What is the average placement rate at MCET?**
A. MCET consistently maintains an average placement rate of 80% to 90%+.
